# Product Brief

## Working name
ClubMatch (working title; branding can change later).

## One sentence
ClubMatch helps Iowa State students quickly discover academic and professional student organizations that actually fit their major, career interests, goals, and schedule.

## Problem
Iowa State has a very large number of student organizations. A traditional directory answers **"what clubs exist?"** but still leaves a student with the harder decision: **"which clubs make sense for me?"**

Students may care about:
- relevance to their major
- career value
- networking
- hands-on experience
- leadership opportunities
- social/community fit
- competitiveness / application requirements
- weekly time commitment
- whether freshmen are welcome
- upcoming meeting information

## Product thesis
The app should reduce choice overload by turning a large catalog into a small, understandable set of personalized recommendations.

## Primary user
An Iowa State student, particularly a newer student, who wants to get involved but does not already know which organizations to pursue.

## Secondary user
Club leaders who want better-qualified interest from students. This matters to the overall business experiment, but **club-leader administration is outside the 24-hour build scope** unless specifically requested.

## Core value proposition
"Tell us what matters to you. We'll surface the clubs that fit."

## Demo success
Someone unfamiliar with the product can:
1. open the app
2. complete the match flow in roughly 45-60 seconds
3. understand why several clubs were recommended
4. open a club page
5. understand what the club does, why it fits, expected commitment, and how to take the next step
