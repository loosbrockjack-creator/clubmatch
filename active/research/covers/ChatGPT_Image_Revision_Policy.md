# ChatGPT image revision policy

Updated: November 7, 2026. Source: Jack Loosbrock's instructions in the ClubMatch image-generation conversation.

## Scope: ChatGPT only

This policy governs ChatGPT's generation, visual review, and regeneration of ClubMatch cover illustrations. It is not an instruction to Claude or Claude Code, and it does not constrain Claude's website implementation, bug fixes, image importing, or repository maintenance. It does not authorize production publishing.

## Revision threshold

Only regenerate or edit an image for a blatant, material problem that substantially harms activity plausibility, club relevance, or usability. Preserve minor imperfections rather than spending another generation for little incremental value.

Examples that can justify a revision:
- An unmistakably impossible activity, severely broken anatomy, or unusable equipment interaction.
- A clearly wrong club activity, misleading subject, or protected Greek artwork being replaced.
- A broken crop or other major issue that makes the cover unusable.

Examples that do not justify an unsolicited revision:
- A small camera-angle adjustment.
- Minor differences in distant architecture or fountain details.
- Small aesthetic refinements or other nitpicks with little effect at card size.

In particular, the previous Astrophotography and Juggling refinements were below the user's desired threshold. Do not use those as the standard for future automatic corrections.

Explicit user requests to revise a particular image take precedence. Existing images do not need another revision because of this policy. Continue reviewing outputs, but record minor observations instead of automatically regenerating them.
